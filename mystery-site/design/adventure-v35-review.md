# v35 — Objects, sequential mechanisms and endings

Ten existing themes now use different ordered plans of physical mechanisms in place of the repeated extra-seal question bundle. Eight interaction types: wire terminals, moving inspection light, photo fragments, receiver tuning, aligning transparent layers, rotating concentric plates, moving a marble through an adjacent-cell maze, and balancing removable weights. Opening one mechanism reveals the next; drafts and completed mechanisms persist per theme.

Stars 1–5 require 2–6 mechanisms. Higher levels also change port count, photo grid size/rotation, hidden marks, maze walls, weights and alignment tolerance. Hints use the existing theme-specific devices with S01–S06 codes. Findings still contribute story context; the new ending choice is an action with the restored object rather than a graded accusation form.

All ten themes have an original generated photoreal ending image, four manually advanced caption beats, and a hidden follow-up trace. The last beat reflects the chosen action; Ohang retains its birth-symbol and player-choice ending. This is a browser scene presentation with subtle camera movement, not a prerecorded video. Original detailed epilogues remain available. Ending art has 480px/960px WebP variants and loads only at the relevant stage.

Ohang's direction exercise now lets players pick up and place bowls. Touch/keyboard alternatives exist for inspection light, sliders, marble movement and concentric plates. New mechanism surfaces override legacy document paper styles to preserve contrast. Existing theme entry puzzles and original studio photography actions remain in the progression.

Validation: all 10 theme plans × 5 levels are solvable in the rule audit; empty, duplicate, incorrect and teleport states are rejected. Entrance/item state, studio save migration, Ohang birth/choice logic and background music audits passed. Browser playthrough: Garage ★ to exit/choice/four-beat ending; Ohang ★★★ through bowl placement, balance, plates, alignment, light, original reveal and choice-dependent ending. Ohang was verified at 390×844 with no horizontal overflow. Reload retained both classic progress and new mechanism progress. Rotating plates gained separate controls after a mobile overlap was found.

References for physical interaction and progression only; stories and implementations are original:

Remaining scope: all 50 theme/level combinations have rule checks, but only the two named combinations were manually played end to end in the browser. More player feedback is needed on pacing and perceived difficulty. Existing completed saves stay completed; use each theme's restart option to experience its new mechanisms.
