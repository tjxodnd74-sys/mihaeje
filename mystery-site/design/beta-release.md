# Private free beta, v24

The existing owner and invited viewer audience is retained. This release does not enable public access, payments, accounts, or collection endpoints.

- Mystery: 6 games, including **자정의 무인 세탁소**, opening the **자정 이후** series
- Heartwarming: 3 games, including **고양이의 초대장**, episode 2 of **돌아오는 발자국**
- Each new game has an independent save key and 7 discoverable records; its final record is sealed until its puzzle is solved
- Recorded sound additions: closing doors, keys, physical locks and radio switches; two paper recordings remain quieter than doors
- Feedback can be opened from the lobby footer or in-game hints and copied for sharing with the inviter; it is never silently transmitted

Validation: all existing game audits passed; new clock checked against 30,000 candidates, seating against 120 arrangements and 10,000 codes; both new games completed through their actual browser controls; cat seating/code survived reload at 390×844; feedback copy confirmed; 44px entry hotspots checked for overlap

Suggested first test: invite 5–10 people after the owner chooses access scope, have them play on their own phones without walkthrough help, and collect the point where they got stuck, control confusion and whether they wanted the next episode

New images were generated as 6 separate scenes/objects, visually inspected and delivered as responsive WebP assets. No new paid sound service or external feedback backend is required.
