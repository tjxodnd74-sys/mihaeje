## Limits
Automated rules and representative mobile interaction do not establish entertainment quality. Ten themes have not all been played to completion on real phones by external testers. The source keeps previous completed progress; old version-one supplemental drafts restart only the new supplemental sequence. No production saves were reset during testing. Five-star notes and paper placement still need player feedback for difficulty and tactile feel.

The audience remains private with the existing invited visitor; no sharing policy changes are made.
